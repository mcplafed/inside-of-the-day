INSIDE OF THE DAY #3

When is an autonomous coding session actually done?

Not when the current PRs are merged.

Merged PRs only tell you that previously dispatched work is finishing.
They do not tell you whether useful work remains.

The real stopping condition is simpler:

The ready issue queue is empty.

Until then, the system should keep doing useful work:

- claim the next ready issue;
- prioritize bugs first;
- refill parallel work as agents finish;
- turn newly discovered follow-ups into ready issues;
- stop only when there is nothing safe and actionable left.

PR count measures a delivery stage.
A ready queue measures remaining opportunity.

For autonomous systems, confusing the two is how productive capacity quietly turns into idle time.
