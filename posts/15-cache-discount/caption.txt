Your AI bill has a hidden discount.

Up to 90% off cached input. Are you comparing cache prices too?

When your apps reuse the same instructions, tool definitions or documents, cached input can be a big part of the bill. And newer models can be much cheaper here too.

A few swaps worth testing today. Cache reads per 1M tokens:

• Claude Haiku 4.5 → 5.5: $0.10 → $0.01 (−90%*)
• GPT-5.4 mini → GPT-6 Luna: $0.075 → $0.01 (−87%)
• GPT-5.4 → GPT-6.1 Terra: $0.25 → $0.05 (−80%)
• GPT-5.5 → GPT-6.1 Sol: $0.50 → $0.10 (−80%)
• Claude Sonnet 4.5 → 5.5: $0.30 → $0.10 (−67%)

The cache needs to warm up after a switch, and writes have their own price. The actual saving depends on how often your prompts are reused, cache hits, output tokens and retries.

So don’t just compare the price list. Test on your own traffic and measure the cost per successful task.

Open your gateway’s usage report. How much are you paying to read the same context again?

*Haiku 5.5 price applies to prompts up to 100k tokens. Standard API list rates, per 1M cache-read tokens, checked 18 November 2026. Percentages rounded. Tokenization and cache rules differ between models.
