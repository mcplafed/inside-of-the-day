# Inside of the Day #1 - Sonnet 5 high vs Opus 4.8 low

## Claim

For Anthropic's updated BrowseComp evaluation, the `Opus 4.8 x low effort` operating point is both lower-cost and slightly higher-scoring than `Sonnet 5 x high effort`.

## Scope

This is an agentic-search benchmark result, not a universal statement about coding agents. The intended takeaway is to benchmark model, effort, and harness combinations in the target workflow.
