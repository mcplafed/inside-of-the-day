INSIDE OF THE DAY #1

I switched my coding agent from Sonnet 5 high to Opus 4.8 low.

It got cheaper - and better.

Anthropic's updated BrowseComp chart shows a counterintuitive operating point:

- Opus 4.8 at low effort: about 78% pass rate at roughly $4-5 per task
- Sonnet 5 at high effort: about 77% pass rate at roughly $5-6 per task

For that benchmark, Sonnet high is dominated: more spend for a slightly worse result.

This is not a universal claim about coding agents. BrowseComp measures agentic search, and outcomes depend on the agent harness: tools, compaction, context, task budget, and prompts.

But it is a useful lesson:

Do not compare model names.
Compare model x effort x your harness.

High effort is not automatically higher quality. Sometimes it is just a more expensive way to get a worse result.
