INSIDE OF THE DAY #4

One small rule in my global CLAUDE.md:

Do not add AI or agent attribution to commits and PRs.

No "Co-Authored-By" trailer.
No "Generated with..." footer.
No session provenance appended to the change.

This is not about hiding the tools used to build software.

It is about making code review start in the right place:

- the diff;
- the reasoning;
- the tests;
- the CI result;
- the operational consequence.

An AI attribution label can become a cognitive shortcut before review even begins. It can create instant distrust - or unearned trust - before anyone has evaluated the change itself.

A PR is a proposal. Its quality should be established by review and evidence, not by a footer.

The human owns the change. The assistant is a tool, not a co-author.

Keep the review focused on the work that will ship.
