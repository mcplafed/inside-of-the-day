INSIDE OF THE DAY #4
An AI attribution footer changes review behavior before anyone reads the code.
It can trigger instant distrust—or false confidence—before the diff is examined.
The better standard is simple: assess the work before its provenance.
Review the change, the reasoning, the tests, the CI result, and the operational consequence.

That is why I do not add AI or agent attribution to commits and PRs:
- no "Co-Authored-By" trailer;
- no "Generated with..." footer;
- no session provenance appended to the change.

This is not about hiding the tools used to build software.
It is about preventing a label from becoming a shortcut around evidence-based review.

A PR is a proposal. Its quality should be established by review and evidence, not by a footer.
The human owns the change. The assistant is a tool, not a co-author.

Keep the review focused on the work that will ship.
