# Inside of the Day #4 - Review the work, not the AI label

## Mechanism

Review the diff, reasoning, tests, CI outcome, and operational consequence. Do not let an AI or agent attribution footer replace evidence-based review.

## Takeaway

A pull request is a proposal. Its quality is established by review and evidence, not provenance labels.
